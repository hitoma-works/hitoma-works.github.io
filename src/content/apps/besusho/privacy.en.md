hitoma works ("we") sets out below how information is handled in KeepRoll (<span lang="ja" style="white-space: nowrap">ベスショ</span> in Japan), our iPhone app ("the app").

## 1. Collection of personal information

The app does not collect any information that can identify you, such as your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or device identifiers. There is no account to sign up for.

## 2. Your photos and videos

The app asks for permission to access your photo library so that it can find similar photos, duplicates, and so on.

- All analysis (similar photos, duplicates, screenshots, large videos, blurry or dark photos, and saved images) happens on your iPhone. Your photos and videos are never sent to our servers or to any other third party.
- Analysis results and your sorting history are stored only on your iPhone. They are removed when you delete the app.
- The app deletes only the photos you choose and approve in the iOS confirmation. Deleted photos move to Recently Deleted in the Photos app.

## 3. Network use

The app connects to the internet only in the following cases:

- When loading small previews of photos that are stored in iCloud Photos and whose originals are not on your iPhone (using Apple's system)
- When you make a purchase or restore a purchase (using the App Store's system)

We do not run our own servers, and we never receive your payment information (such as card numbers). Information in these cases is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy.

<ul class="link-list">
  <li><a href="https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/">Apple Privacy Policy</a></li>
</ul>

## 4. What is stored in iCloud

The number of photos you have deleted for free is stored on your iPhone and in your own iCloud (Apple's key-value storage). This is so that the count does not reset when you reinstall the app. We cannot see this information.

## 5. Analytics and advertising

The app does not use analytics tools that collect usage data, crash-reporting tools, or advertising.

## 6. Changes

If we change this policy, we will announce it on this page and update the "Last updated" date.
