## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>I deleted photos, but my free space didn't increase</summary>

Photos you delete with the app stay in Recently Deleted in the Photos app for 30 days, and they keep using space during that time. To get the space back right away, open Albums → Recently Deleted in the Photos app and delete them there.

If you use iCloud Photos, the space freed is mainly in iCloud. On your iPhone, space is freed only for photos whose originals are on your iPhone.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I deleted photos by mistake</summary>

Within 30 days of deleting them, you can recover them from Albums → Recently Deleted in the Photos app. They can't be recovered from within the app.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What can I use for free?</summary>

Checking (finding similar photos, duplicates, screenshots, and so on) is always free. You can delete up to 100 photos in total for free.

Beyond that, a one-time purchase lets you delete without limits. It's a one-time payment, with no trial period and no automatic charges. Family Sharing is supported.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I want to restore my purchase</summary>

In the app, go to Settings and tap "Restore Purchase". You need to be signed in on your iPhone with the same Apple Account you used to make the purchase.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I allowed access to only some of my photos</summary>

In that case, the app checks only the photos you allowed. You can change which photos are selected under Photo Access in the app's Settings. To check all of your photos, go to Settings → Apps → KeepRoll → Photos on your iPhone and choose "Full Access".

</details>

<details>
<summary>Are my photos ever sent anywhere?</summary>

No. All analysis happens on your iPhone, and your photos and videos are never sent anywhere. For details, see the <a href="/apps/besusho/en/privacy/">Privacy Policy</a>.

</details>
